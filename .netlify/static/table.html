Round the Table

One phone, passed around. Everyone picks a secret key at the start, and your hand only appears when your own key is tapped — so the phone can go round the table without anyone seeing anybody else's cards.

  • · Two, three or four players; the computer fills any empty seat.
  • · Nobody else knows your key, so nobody else can open your hand.
  • · When only one card is legal, it is played for you and the phone stays put.
