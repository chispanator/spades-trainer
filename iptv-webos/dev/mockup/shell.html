Things to try

  1. Movies → Uncategorized: 12 titles most players would hide.
  2. Info → Deep scan Movies: finds 5 titles the server left out of the full list.
  3. Live TV: open a channel, then CH + / − to flip channels. "Offline Test Channel" shows the error screen.
  4. Series: pick a show, choose a season and play an episode.
  5. Search: type "amelie" to find "Amélie Returns".
  6. Press ★ on anything to add it to Favorites.

Keyboard

  • ← ↑ → ↓ move, Enter is OK
  • Esc is Back, F is ★ favorite
  • PgUp / PgDn are CH + / −
